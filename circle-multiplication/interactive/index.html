THE BEAUTY BEHIND IT

No curves drawn.
Yet curves emerge.

Place N points evenly around a circle, numbered from 0. Choose a multiplier m, then connect each point i to (i × m) mod N. When the count goes beyond one full turn, wrap around to the beginning.

i ⟶ (i × m) mod N

For example, with N = 240 and m = 2, point 10 connects to point 20, and point 130 also connects to point 20. Together, the straight lines trace the outline of a cardioid. When the multiplier is not a whole number, the endpoints move smoothly around the circle and the pattern changes with them.

↗ A small challengeSet the point count to 20 and count the connections. Then increase it to 600 and watch the outline emerge.
