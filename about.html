Through my lens

Photography

◎Photography ↗

My camera is my second pair of eyes. Through photography, I collect not only images, but also stories, emotions, and perspectives. Travel keeps me curious, and my lens helps me remember the moments that might otherwise disappear. Even amid the intense academic workload of Grades 10 and 11, I continued my journey of exploration — from the streets of Beijing and Tokyo to the landscapes of Xinjiang and the coastline of Malibu. Every place I have visited has left a mark, not only on my camera roll, but also on the way I see the world.
