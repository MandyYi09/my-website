Tennis / the practice of showing up

Before,
I hesitate.

There is usually a moment before practice when I want to stay home or keep studying. I know that feeling well enough now that I try not to treat it as a decision. I simply go.

“The hardest part is often just starting.”
Rowing / returning to the present

During,
I return.

Once I start moving, schoolwork and deadlines move into the background. In rowing, repetition and rhythm make my attention settle into the body instead of the noise around it.

“Movement gives my mind somewhere to be.”
